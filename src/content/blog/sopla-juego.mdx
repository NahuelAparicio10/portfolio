###  **About**
<div className="text-justify">

In El Sopla Juego, a **3D FPS**, the player steps into the shoes of a garbage collector armed with a unique weapon: a **vacuum cleaner** that can suck, blow, and shoot absorbed objects. After breaking a neighbor’s garden gnome, the protagonist is forced to repay the debt by completing a variety of missions across the city.

This project was developed as a final degree project in collaboration with a team of artists and musicians. I joined as the **sole programmer**, responsible for turning the team’s creative vision into a functional game. My work focused on designing and implementing all gameplay systems while integrating artistic and audio assets into Unity, ensuring a cohesive and playable prototype.
</div>
